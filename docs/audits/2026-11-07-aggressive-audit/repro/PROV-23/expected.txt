Spec: SP 800-90B 5.1 Figure 4 (PDF p. 37): "2. For j = 1 to 10 000 ... 2.2.2
If (T > Ti), increment Ci,0. If (T = Ti), increment Ci,1. 3. If ((Ci,0 + Ci,1
<= 5) or (Ci,0 >= 9995)) for any i, reject the IID assumption".

The counters the standard defines are out of exactly 10,000. The fork's early
termination (permutation_tests.h:672-674: stop a statistic once C0+C1 > 5 and
C1+C2 > 5) is verdict-equivalent (proof in logs/analysis/iid-permutation.md
section 2.1) -- the VERDICT is not the defect.

Expected JSON: either counters that are out of 10,000, or the partial
counters together with the number of permutations evaluated for that
statistic (e.g. "permutationsEvaluated": 45), so a consumer can form the rank.

Observed: "permutationTestResults" holds three objects ("iteration" 0, 1, 2
= C0, C1, C2) whose sums are a few dozen for every passing statistic (e.g.
avgCollision 6/0/39 = 45 of 10,000 in the analysis probe) and 10,000 only for
a statistic that fails; nothing names the denominator. A consumer reading C0
as "permutations that exceeded the original, out of 10,000" misreads the rank
by two orders of magnitude. Direction: neutral (verdict unchanged). LOW.
