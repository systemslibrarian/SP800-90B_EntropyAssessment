## Verdict and interpretation

The previous review's defect and attribution to F19 are **CONFIRMED**, including at the recorded current tip. This is a build portability regression, not evidence that F19's subset arithmetic correction is mathematically wrong. Status direction: OVERSTATED for any unqualified ordinary-GCC build/suite success claim. No claim is made that the macOS build fails.

Any subsequent GCC functional build will explicitly add `-include climits` as a command-line-only diagnostic adapter. Such a run does not make the original unmodified build pass. Reviewed files remain unmodified.

No timeouts, sanitizer suppressions, or failed compilation counted as behavioral mutation sensitivity in this finding.
