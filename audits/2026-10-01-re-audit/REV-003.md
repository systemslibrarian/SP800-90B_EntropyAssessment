## Independent reference, not an AI expected-value oracle

The local NIST PDF was SHA-256-verified against the repository manifest and §6.3.9 pp52–55 read completely. The new Python reference implements the literal order: update **previous** transitions for all orders, predict with greatest-symbol tie breaking, choose the previously winning subpredictor, record correctness, then update scoreboards. Unlike the optimized target it does not preinitialize future transitions, interleave current updates, reuse PostfixDictionary, or skip chained lookups. It was checked against the standard's published worked example: correct=(0,0,1,1,0,1,0), C=3, longest run=2.

The standard's printed **r is one greater than the longest run**, which the target's predictionEstimate also prints. Thus the reported binary r=17 means a longest correct run of 16, not 17 consecutive correct predictions.
